# Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated / 最後更新：2026-10-30

## English

Stillgate does not collect, sell or share your personal data, and it has no ads or analytics.

- **Network use.** The app declares the INTERNET and billing permissions only because Google Play's billing component needs them to show subscription prices and confirm purchases for the optional Pro subscription. Stillgate itself does not send your locked-app list, usage counts, typed reasons or anything the accessibility service sees.
- **What it reads.** Through the Android accessibility service it receives only "a window changed" events and the package name of the app in front (for example `com.example.app`). It does not read screen content and does not record anything you type in other apps.
- **What it stores, on your phone only.** The list of apps you chose to lock, your settings, today's open counts and usage minutes, the reasons you typed on the pause screen, and whether your Pro subscription was last confirmed active. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.
- **Payments.** Subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. Stillgate never receives your card or payment details; it only learns whether a subscription is active.
- **Third parties.** None. There are no ads, analytics or tracking libraries.
- **Turning it off.** You can disable the accessibility service at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/Ben0126/stillgate-privacy/issues

## 繁體中文（台灣）

Stillgate 不會蒐集、出售或分享你的個人資料，也沒有廣告或分析程式。

- **網路用途。** App 宣告了 INTERNET 與付款權限，只是因為 Google Play 的付款元件需要它們，才能顯示訂閱價格並確認選用的 Pro 訂閱。Stillgate 本身不會傳送你鎖定的 App 清單、使用次數、你寫的理由，或無障礙服務看到的任何內容。
- **讀取什麼。** 透過 Android 無障礙服務，只會收到「視窗切換了」的通知，以及最前面 App 的套件名稱（例如 `com.example.app`）。不會讀取畫面內容，也不會記錄你在其他 App 輸入的任何內容。
- **儲存什麼（只存在你的手機上）。** 你選擇鎖定的 App 清單、你的設定、今天的開啟次數與使用分鐘、你在等待畫面寫下的理由，以及 Pro 訂閱上次確認有效的時間。解除安裝後全部刪除。
- **付款。** 訂閱由 Google Play 販售與處理。Stillgate 不會取得你的信用卡或付款資料，只會知道訂閱是否有效。
- **第三方。** 沒有。沒有廣告、分析或追蹤程式庫。
- **關閉方式。** 你可以隨時到「設定 → 無障礙」關閉無障礙服務。

聯絡方式：到 https://github.com/Ben0126/stillgate-privacy/issues 開 issue
